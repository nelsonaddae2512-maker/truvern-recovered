# 01 - Executive Summary

Owner: Truvern Leadership
Status: Baseline
Version: 1.0
Last Updated: 2026-07-23

Truvern is a third-party governance and vendor-risk platform combining vendor
management, assessments, evidence, review workflows, findings, remediation,
communications, credit-funded professional reviews, immutable governance
releases, and board-ready reporting.

Review paths:
1. Self-Managed Review
2. Hybrid review routed to Truvern
3. End-to-end Truvern Review

Commercial model: Free, Pro, and Enterprise platform access. Truvern Review is
separately funded through credits unless an explicit unlimited agreement exists.
