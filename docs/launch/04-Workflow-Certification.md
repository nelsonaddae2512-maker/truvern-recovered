# 04 - Workflow Certification

Owner: Truvern QA and Product
Status: Not Certified
Version: 1.0
Last Updated: 2026-07-23

Launch-critical workflows include onboarding, vendor creation, all three review
paths, credits, questionnaire delivery, evidence, response-grounded findings,
remediation, approval, immutable release, board packets, communications, and
tenant isolation. Each certification must record preconditions, success and
failure paths, recovery, audit events, evidence, tester, date, and result.
